          Sources & how to read the signals

          News: about 120 Google News searches (one per competitor, project searches for each market, in English, Arabic, French and German), the EU (TED) and Canadian (CanadaBuys) tender databases, and five trade feeds (SaudiGulf Projects, GCC Tender News, Utilities Middle East, offshoreWIND.biz, Electrical Business), refreshed daily and matched by keyword. Deal stage and value are read from the headline, and some items will be off-topic or missed, so always confirm against the source. Projects: curated by hand in data/projects.json. Prices: LME official cash settlement, 3-month and warehouse stock (USD/t) via westmetall.com, each business day.
